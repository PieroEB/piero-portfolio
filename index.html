GridLock

Finding coordination opportunities across electric utilities' construction plans.

Utilities publish future transmission and substation projects in long, inconsistent PDFs. GridLock lines up the plans of Dominion Energy SC and Georgia Power to surface projects within 25 miles of each other that could share crews, equipment, or rights-of-way, without inventing matches.

252project candidates extracted from utility PDFs
45records validated with page-level source evidence

My role

  • Built the React + Leaflet frontend: the interactive project map, project details, side-by-side comparison with a 25-mile screening check, and the recommendation views.
  • Connected the frontend to the team's recommendation agent and added an offline fallback so the demo works without the API.
  • Built the Gemini extraction pipeline with a strict JSON schema, page-level evidence, and resumable batches.
  • Geolocated substations with OpenStreetMap, cross-checked against the federal HIFLD dataset, and validated every record against its source.

CipherQ

What can quantum results actually prove? Two labs built on honest verification.

Policy Lab compiles access-control rules into a quantum oracle and runs Grover's algorithm (Qiskit) to search for policy violations, tested on IonQ Aria 1 simulators. Channel Detective simulates the BB84 key-distribution protocol to show why transmission errors alone don't prove an eavesdropper.

95.5%of quantum shots landed on the policy violation
6.25%what random guessing would get (1 of 16)

My role

  • Wrote the exact classical evaluator that verifies every Grover measurement, with 10 unit tests (merged PR).
  • Built the BB84 evidence lab: error rates, confidence intervals, and sample-size sweeps with cautious conclusions.
  • Wrote the demo cases and judge script: observation vs. hypothesis vs. conclusion.
